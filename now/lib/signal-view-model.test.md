# Signal view-model acceptance cases

1. FREE + two fresh matching answers → positive tone and confidence at or above 50%.
2. SMALL + one fresh answer → muted/insufficient signal, never a strong queue claim.
3. LARGE + two fresh matching answers → negative tone.
4. Equal FREE/SMALL counts → UNKNOWN/neutral, because the signal is tied.
5. Only stale answers → UNKNOWN with `Ждём свежие ответы`.
6. Unknown answer text must not become a queue status.
7. Four fresh answers → subtitle uses the correct plural form.
